Words fall from a quiet galaxy. Type a word to lock on — each correct
letter charges a laser; finish the word and the alien bursts. Wrong
keys shake the chip, hesitate and the line breaks: three crossings and
the run ends. Combo multiplies every kill; the sky thickens as you score.
